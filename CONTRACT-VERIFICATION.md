What PQTABS enforces, and what it inherits:

- PQTABS, in the matched PQRoot source: SLH-DSA authorization, nonce, deadline, exposure ceiling, open and close accounting, permissionless reclaim after expiry, and the factory pin to the Barkeep addresses above.
- Barkeep, in unverified bytecode: the capability's agent, payee list, per-call maximum, expiry, and the signature check on a spend. PQTABS tests that path with a mock, not with this bytecode. The live PQRoot does not read `agent()`, `payees()`, or `maxPerCall()` back after `openTab`. PQRootNext does. Existing clones still execute PQRoot.
